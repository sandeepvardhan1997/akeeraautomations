What we actually do

Three problems, over and over. If one of these sounds like your office, we can help.

Your staff send numbers on WhatsApp at night and you believe whatever arrives. We put check-in, live location and every visit on record — from their own phone, no extra device.

Questions owners ask first

Will it suit our industry?

Akeera Field is not built for one trade. You choose your line of business in Settings and the app renames itself around it — a coaching institute works with students, admissions and fees, a distributor with dealers, orders and collections, a clinic with patients and appointments. Underneath it is the same system, so you get the same tracking, pipeline and collection screens whatever you pick. Try it on the free trial and see how it reads for your business.

Do we have to throw away the software we use now?

Usually not. Most of what we build sits on Google Workspace and Sheets you already pay for, and connects to your existing Tally or billing software rather than replacing it. If something genuinely needs replacing, we say so before you spend anything.

Our staff are not technical. Will they use it?

That is the main design constraint, not an afterthought. Field staff use one screen on their own phone. Office staff work in the sheet format they already know. We train the team in person and leave documentation in simple language.

Who owns the data and the system?

You do. Everything is built inside your own Google or cloud account, in your name. You keep full access to the data and the code, and you are never locked to us to keep it running.

Can we pay monthly instead of all at once?

Yes. Every custom plan shows a monthly figure next to the one-time price, and Akeera Field is monthly by default. Most owners trying us for the first time start monthly. There is no lock-in either way.

Do you charge GST, and will we get a proper invoice?

Yes to both. We are GST registered, and every payment comes with a proper tax invoice carrying our GSTIN. If your business is registered too, the 18% is input credit you can claim back.

How long before something actually works?

Akeera Field works the same day you sign up. A custom build has its first live module running on your real data in two to three weeks; a full ERP takes longer and is delivered in stages, so you are never waiting months to see anything.
