Questions owners usually ask first

Do we have to throw away the software we use now?

Usually not. Most of what we build sits on Google Workspace, Sheets and tools you are already paying for, and connects to your existing Tally or billing software rather than replacing it. If something genuinely needs replacing, we will say so before you spend anything.

Who owns the data and the system?

You do. Everything is built inside your own Google or cloud account, in your name. You keep full access to the data and the code, and you are never locked to us to keep it running.

Our staff are not technical. Will they use it?

That is the main design constraint, not an afterthought. Field staff use one screen on their own phone. Office staff work in the sheet format they already know. We train the team in person and leave documentation in simple language.

How long before something actually works?

The first live module is usually running on your real data in two to three weeks. A full ERP or CRM rollout takes longer and is delivered in stages, so you are never waiting months to see anything.

Why is every price marked "onwards"?

Because a five-person trading firm and a hundred-person factory need very different systems, and quoting one number for both would mean overcharging one of you. The plans show where each level starts. After the first call you get a single fixed figure in writing, and it does not move afterwards.

Is the monthly support plan compulsory?

No. Every project includes a support window after handover, and the system keeps running whether or not you continue. The monthly plan is for businesses that want ongoing changes, new reports and additions as they grow.

What happens if something breaks later?

During your support window, we fix it at no extra cost. After that, changes and fixes are covered by the monthly plan, or quoted separately if you are not on one.
